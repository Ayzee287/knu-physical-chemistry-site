# 0003 — Design direction

Date: 2026-06-10 · Status: accepted

## Decision

Inherit the faculty project's visual family — ivory paper ground, deep academic
navy, restrained sand, slate body text, Cormorant→Lora serif headings with
Inter body — and differentiate the department register with two moves:

1. **`ink` (#0f1b36)** — a deeper base surface used for the institutional hero
   (the one deliberately atmospheric moment); content pages stay on paper tones.
2. **`copper` (#9a6a3f)** replaces the faculty's gold as the single accent —
   eyebrows, hairlines, scope-note rules. More material and laboratory-toned,
   used with the same discipline gold had.

Composition rules carried over: typography-first identity, hairline-separated
editorial rows instead of floating cards, documentary (reserved) figure plates,
no gradients, no decorative animation beyond hover affordances.

## Why

The department site must read as part of the same institution as the faculty
site while feeling more scientific and research-oriented. Changing one surface
and one accent achieves that without forking the design system.
