# 0005 — Operator-authorized publication of the curated set

Date: 2026-06-10 · Status: accepted · Amends ADR-0001 for featured records

## Decision

Featured person records now publish with their **sourced** provenance — the
verified-only rule of ADR-0001 is amended for the curated set, on explicit
operator authorization (session directive, 2026-06-10). The curated public
set is: **dean, head of department, and key faculty (Іщенко, Олексенко,
Роїк, Усенко)**. Research-group leaders may additionally appear in the
group-designation form («група проф. N») on the research surface only.

Review marks continue to show the trust state of every published claim in
review mode; flipping a claim to `verified(...)` remains the goal of the
verification backlog and removes the marks.

## Why

- Four to five convergent sources now corroborate these records (v1/v2
  faculty directories, the legacy department site, and the v3 department
  profile citing the university's current information systems —
  infopacket.knu.ua, vstup.chem.knu.ua).
- The operator, as responsible owner, explicitly directed publication of the
  named set. An institutional site whose dean and head are "to be confirmed"
  indefinitely fails its purpose; the residual risk is now an editorial
  decision taken by the responsible human, recorded here.

## Boundaries that still hold

- Internal records (remaining docents, vice-deans) stay unpublished.
- Volatile biography statistics (h-index, publication counts, prize lists)
  are NOT published — they live in source records only.
- New facts still enter collections as `sourced` with citation + retrieval
  date; nothing is laundered into editorial text.
